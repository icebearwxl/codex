Words that unlock a room

German that works.

You do not need perfect German to begin living in German. Learn the sentences that keep a conversation going, then add campus and Behörden language as your life expands.

Survival German

Three sentences that buy you time.

Start with phrases that unlock a conversation: “Could you repeat that slowly?”, “I have an appointment”, “I received this letter”. A weekly language tandem, campus group or part-time job adds repetition that classes alone may not.

Können Sie das bitte langsamer wiederholen?

Please repeat that more slowly.

Ich habe einen Termin.

I have an appointment.

Ich habe diesen Brief bekommen.

I received this letter.

Sprechen Sie Englisch?

Useful — but try the German first. People often meet you halfway.

Supermarket German

Checkout is a short script.

Learn bag, receipt, card and bottle-return words. Cashiers often speak quickly; having one polite sentence ready is more useful than a vocabulary list you never say out loud.

  • Tüte — bag. Ich brauche eine Tüte, bitte.
  • Quittung / Bon — receipt.
  • Pfand — bottle deposit, returned at the machine.
  • Karte, bitte — card, please.

Housing German

Contracts are a language of their own.

Before you sign, be able to ask about Warmmiete, Kaution, notice period and whether Anmeldung is allowed. Write the questions down; viewing apartments is noisy and rushed.

Behörden German

Offices reward preparation, not fluency.

Bring documents in order, say why you are there in one sentence, and ask where to wait. Ich möchte mich anmelden. / Ich brauche einen Termin. / Welche Unterlagen fehlen noch? are often enough to start the interaction.

University German

Campus German is a second dialect.

Enrolment, exam registration and office hours have their own verbs. Pair this page with the university guide, and use your university email in German when you can — it is low-stakes practice with a real purpose.
