Campus · Self-organisation

University, without guessing.

German universities expect you to register, deregister and read the rules yourself. The examination regulations are more useful than guessing from other students’ experience.

RPTU

A campus that becomes a second city.

The Rhineland-Palatinate Technical University’s Kaiserslautern campus is a major anchor for student life, research and events. Learn the bus route, the library, your department building and the International Affairs office before the first deadline cluster arrives.

Courses

Read the rules before the semester accelerates.

Course registration, exam registration and deregistration may be separate systems with separate deadlines. Download the examination regulations and module handbook for your programme.

  • Use the university email address and check it regularly.
  • Ask Fachschaft (student representatives) when an administrative instruction is unclear.
  • Start assignments early: libraries and supervisors work on their own schedules.

Exams

Attending is not the same as being registered.

Register for exams yourself; sitting in the lecture may not be enough. Note the deregistration window. If you are unsure, ask the Prüfungsamt in writing so you have a record.

Student life

Join one thing that is not a lecture.

Sports courses, Fachschaft, language tandems and Chinese student groups turn a quiet city into a network. One recurring weekly commitment is often more useful than a long list of clubs you never visit.

Useful resources

Bookmark the official pages, then this guide.

University portals, the semester ticket explanation, library account, and International Affairs pages change less painfully if you save them on day one. This site stays a planning companion — not a replacement for the current official text.
