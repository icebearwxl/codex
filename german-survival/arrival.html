MODULE 02 / THE FIRST MONTH

02

Arrival &
admin.

The first week is not about finishing everything. It is about getting the correct foundations so the next appointments become possible.

02.1

THE ORDER MATTERS

A calm first-week sequence.

  1. Secure a usable address. Ask whether your landlord can issue a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung; without it, address registration may be impossible.
  2. Register your address. Book an Anmeldung appointment at the Bürgeramt. Bring passport, rental contract and the landlord confirmation.
  3. Get the registration certificate. Keep the Meldebescheinigung safe; bank, tax and residence-permit steps may ask for it.
  4. Open your everyday banking route. Check passport/visa requirements and how your blocked-account money will reach it.
Save every letter.

German paperwork often arrives by post. Create one physical folder and one scanned folder; do not throw away a letter just because you do not understand it yet.

02.2

INSURANCE & RESIDENCE

Understand the difference
between proof and coverage.

You may have visa-stage insurance and later need student statutory insurance or another accepted option for enrolment. Your university normally explains the electronic insurance notification it requires. Read its enrolment checklist carefully.

Residence permit

If your visa does not cover the entire study period, apply for a residence permit at the local Ausländerbehörde before it expires. Appointment availability can be limited, so retain proof that you requested an appointment and follow local instructions. Expect passport, registration, health insurance, financial proof, enrolment and biometric photo to be relevant.

02.3

A SMALL CHECKLIST

Things that reduce friction.

German SIM / eSIM

A local number makes delivery, banking and appointment messages simpler. Compare data, contract duration and identity verification.

Public transport

Ask your university about the semester ticket and how it is activated. Regional trains, buses and long-distance trains have different rules.

Tax ID

Your Steuer-ID normally comes by post after registration. Store it; employers will ask for it later.

Broadcasting fee

Each household receives a Rundfunkbeitrag obligation. In a WG, coordinate so the household does not pay twice.
