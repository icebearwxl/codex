01.1

ACADEMIC VERIFICATION

APS is your first
hard deadline.

For most applicants educated in mainland China, the APS certificate (Akademische Prüfstelle) verifies that your previous education qualifies you for German study. Universities and visa offices usually need it, so treat it as an early gate—not an item to leave until you have an offer.

Start with the official APS China website.

Requirements, fees, appointment rules and accepted documents can change. Check the current instructions before you notarise or translate anything.

Prepare a clean document set

  • Passport copy and APS application form.
  • Your Gaokao / school credentials and university transcript, as applicable.
  • Official translations where the current APS instructions require them.
  • Proof of application fee and every copy requested in the checklist.

Build one digital folder with clear names: 01_passport.pdf, 02_transcript.pdf. You will reuse them repeatedly.

01.2

CHOOSING WHERE TO STUDY

Research the programme,
not only the city.

Germany has public universities, universities of applied sciences (Hochschule / FH) and private institutions. They can lead to different learning styles and costs. Read a programme’s module handbook, language requirement and admission rules—not just its ranking.

Universität

Usually theory and research-focused. A good fit if you enjoy academic study or may pursue a master’s / PhD.

Hochschule / FH

Often more applied, structured and connected to industry projects. Course formats can be smaller and practical.

A useful research workflow

  1. Use DAAD’s international programme database to find degree programmes.
  2. Open the university’s own programme page and module handbook.
  3. Record language level, semester start, contribution, deadline and portal in one table.
  4. Check subject requirements before applying, especially for master’s degrees.
01.3

APPLICATIONS

Know which door
you are applying through.

Some programmes use uni-assist for preliminary document checking; others accept direct applications through their own portal. A programme might also use Hochschulstart or a special platform. Never assume that one completed application reaches every university.

CHECKWHY IT MATTERS
Deadline typeWinter and summer semesters have different dates; some master’s courses close much earlier.
Language proofGerman courses may request TestDaF, DSH or telc; English courses often request IELTS/TOEFL.
Document formatPortals differ: certified copy, upload, postal mail or later verification.

Apply to a considered set. Keep a tracker with portal login, status, fee and missing documents.

01.4

MONEY & ENTRY

The visa application is
a document story.

After admission, you normally prepare financial proof, health insurance that fits the visa stage, accommodation information and your APS certificate. Many students use a blocked account, but the required monthly amount and accepted evidence are set by authorities and can change.

Before your appointment

  • Use the German mission in China’s current student-visa checklist for your jurisdiction.
  • Confirm the current blocked-account amount and how monthly payments work after arrival.
  • Print admission, financial proof, insurance evidence, passport copies and forms in the requested order.
  • Do not buy non-refundable flights before you understand your visa timeline.
Official sources are the source of truth.

This page is a planning guide. For visa rules, use the relevant German mission and Auswärtiges Amt instructions current on the day you apply.

01.5

48 HOURS BEFORE FLIGHT

Carry the things
you cannot replace.

Keep your passport, visa, admission letter, temporary-address details, insurance documents, cash/card and key contacts in your cabin bag. Save offline copies in secure storage too.
